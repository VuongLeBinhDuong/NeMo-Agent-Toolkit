PROJECT_NAME: project-plan
REQUIREMENTS: This project aims to create a functional e-commerce website for tech gadgets. The website should be user-friendly, responsive, and accessible. The target users are tech enthusiasts and consumers looking for gadgets online. Success criteria include a fully functional website, proper layout, and correct functionality of features.
PRODUCTS: - Product 1: Smartphone, $500, Electronics, A high-end smartphone with advanced features.
- Product 2: Laptop, $1000, Electronics, A high-performance laptop for gaming and work.
- Product 3: Headphones, $200, Electronics, Wireless headphones with long battery life.
CATEGORIES: - Electronics
SORT_OPTIONS: - Price (low to high)
- Price (high to low)
- Alphabetical
FUNCTIONALITY: - Users can filter products by category
- Users can sort products by price or alphabetical order
- Users can search for products by name
- Users can add products to cart and view cart contents
UI_COMPONENTS: - Header with logo, menu, search bar, and cart section
- Hero banner on homepage
- Featured products section on homepage
- Shop page with category filtering and sorting options
- Product detail page with images and specifications
- Cart page with quantity controls and totals
